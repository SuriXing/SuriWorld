In recent times, I had the opportunity to explore the capabilities of DeepSeek, at first, I thought it's just one new AI chatbox that's similar to any of the others (like chatgpt, cici, kimi etc.). But as I chat more with it and actually using it to fix some real problems I have, I must say, the experience has been truly eye-opening. The efficiency and proficiency it demonstrated left a deep impression on me.

For instance, when it came to coding tasks, it only took a matter of seconds along with some detailed descriptions to generate outstanding code. I had been harboring an idea of creating an online version similar to Keigo Higashino's novel "The Miraculous Kiosk of Lost Time", which aimed to assist people in resolving their worries. With just a few simple sentences of explanation, DeepSeek was able to produce relevant content. Initially, the outcome might not have fully met my expectations. However, as I provided more detailed requirements, the results it delivered became increasingly satisfactory, and the overall "product" it created evolved to be better and better.

Moreover, DeepSeek exhibits an astonishing aptitude for solving mathematical problems. During my regular math study sessions, I often encounter difficulties when dealing with incorrect answers. In situations where the correct solutions are not provided, I would typically turn to other AIs like CiCi for assistance. Regrettably, It can't get the correct answer and accurate solutions + explanations for majority of the times. This not only led to confusion but also wasted precious time as I had to double-check and rework the problems. However, after experimenting with DeepSeek, I was pleasantly surprised. So far, it has managed to solve every single math problem I presented it with accurately. Whether it's complex algebraic equations, intricate geometric proofs, or challenging calculus problems, DeepSeek seems to possess an in-depth understanding of mathematical principles and can apply them with precision. It meticulously analyzes the problem statement, identifies the relevant mathematical concepts, and then formulates a step-by-step solution that is not only correct but also presented in a clear and logical manner. This makes it an invaluable tool for students and professionals alike who are grappling with mathematical challenges, providing them with reliable guidance and helping them to build a stronger foundation in the subject.

DeepSeek's reasoning ability is remarkable. In a strategic business context, it can analyze market trends, competitor moves and consumer preferences, predict future changes like a shrewd entrepreneur, and formulate innovative and practical strategies. It also mimics human thinking well. When dealing with complex riddles or philosophical questions, it considers various viewpoints, weighs pros and cons, and reaches surprisingly profound and coherent conclusions.

Nonetheless, its self-evolving trait is a concern. By learning from extensive data, it constantly sharpens its skills. There's a fear that soon, it might encroach on areas reserved for human intellect, from scientific research needing creativity to negotiation relying on emotional intelligence, potentially redefining human dominance and the nature of thinking, creating and contributing.

In conclusion, DeepSeek represents a remarkable advancement in the realm of artificial intelligence. It offers unprecedented convenience and efficiency in various domains, from coding to content creation and now, impressively, in mathematics. While we embrace its potential, we must also approach it with caution and use it as a complementary tool to enhance, rather than replace, human capabilities.
